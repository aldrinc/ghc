# Goal

Create a customer-friendly onboarding path that lets a new or existing business create a workspace, set up a marketing agent, define what the business sells or plans to sell, and prepare internal foundation research/avatar docs with minimal friction.

Key results:

- 7-9 focused screens maximum.
- Price optional.
- Competitor/source URLs optional.
- Existing business supported.
- Existing-business onboarding uses Context.dev from website URL plus optional competitors.
- Existing-business review asks "Does this look right to you?" and lets the customer edit extracted fields.
- New-business onboarding asks "What do you sell or plan to sell?" then product/service-specific questions, including product price vs service charging model.
- Internal foundation docs auto-run without user gates.
- UI frames the experience as marketing-agent setup, not research generation.
- No offer or copy artifacts generated during onboarding.
