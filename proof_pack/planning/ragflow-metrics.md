Leading metrics:
- Percent of target corpus ingested into RAGFlow with metadata and provenance.
- Retrieval hit rate on a fixed eval question set.
- Citation coverage per answer.
- Latency and cost per query by model.
- Parse/index failure rate by document type.

Lagging metrics:
- Answer accuracy versus human-reviewed expected answers.
- Hallucination rate or unsupported-claim rate.
- Task success rate versus Gemini File Search and Claude Projects.
- Operator time saved per workflow.
- Percent of RAG tasks moved to the vendor-neutral API.
