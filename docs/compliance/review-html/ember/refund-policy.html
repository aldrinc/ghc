Refund Policy

30-day return and refund rules for Ember

This draft follows the approved Option A posture: clear and firm enough for operational protection, but more customer-friendly than a strict no-refund supplement policy.

Effective date: April 16, 2026 Operator: Ember Gummies Return window: 30 days

Return window

Eligible returns must be requested within 30 days of delivery.

Requests submitted after the 30-day window may be denied unless required by law or unless Ember determines a customer-service exception is appropriate.

Eligibility for returns

  • Items must be unused, unopened, and in original packaging.
  • Proof of purchase or the order number is required.
  • Opened consumable products are generally not eligible for return.
  • Items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect remain eligible for review even if opened for inspection.

How to start a return

Contact support@shopemberco.com before sending anything back. Please include your order number, the item involved, and the reason for the request.

Unauthorized returns may be refused. If your request is approved, Ember will provide the next steps and, when applicable, the correct return address.

Refund method and timing

Once a return is received and inspected, Ember will notify you whether the refund is approved.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. After approval, financial institutions may take an additional 5 to 10 business days to post the refund.

Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the return is due to an Ember fulfillment error or a verified defective item.

Subscription purchases

Subscription changes or cancellations should be made at least 48 hours before the next renewal date.

  • If a subscription renewal has not yet processed, Ember will honor timely pause or cancellation requests.
  • If a renewal has already processed, that order is generally not retroactively refundable.
  • Defective, damaged, or incorrectly fulfilled subscription orders will still be reviewed case by case.

Exceptions and non-returnable items

  • Opened ingestible or consumable products, except where required by law or where the item is defective or incorrect.
  • Final-sale items or promotional items marked as non-returnable.
  • Gift cards, if offered.
  • Orders showing signs of abuse, resale intent, or repeated policy misuse.
If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact support promptly. Those cases are reviewed separately from standard preference-based returns.

Contact us

Questions about returns, damaged items, or refund timing should be sent to support@shopemberco.com.
