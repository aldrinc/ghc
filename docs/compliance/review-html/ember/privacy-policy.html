Privacy Policy for Ember

Ember Gummies ("Ember", "we", "us", "our") is committed to handling personal information in a way that is transparent, practical, and respectful of customer expectations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and retain information when you visit https://shopemberco.com, purchase products, join a subscription, sign up for marketing, or contact support.

What we collect

Depending on how you interact with Ember, we may collect:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, shipping address, and billing address.
  • Order details such as purchased items, subscription status, transaction information, and order history.
  • Support details such as the contents of emails, chat messages, return requests, and issue reports.
  • Device and browsing data such as IP address, browser type, referring pages, on-site activity, and conversion events.
  • Marketing and attribution data collected through cookies, pixels, tags, and similar tools used to measure campaign performance.
Ember does not sell personal information for cash. We do use standard ecommerce and advertising tools to understand traffic, improve conversion, and communicate with customers more effectively.

How we use information

  • To process payments, fulfill orders, manage subscriptions, and provide customer support.
  • To send order updates, shipping notifications, and service-related messages.
  • To send promotional email or SMS where you have opted in or where allowed by law.
  • To prevent fraud, monitor abuse, and protect the security of our store and customers.
  • To analyze site performance, understand customer behavior, and improve our offers and content.

We may also use aggregated or de-identified data for internal analytics, forecasting, and business planning.

When we share information

We may share information with:

  • Payment processors, ecommerce platforms, fulfillment partners, subscription tools, and support vendors needed to operate the business.
  • Analytics and advertising providers who help us measure traffic, campaigns, and customer engagement.
  • Professional advisors, insurers, or acquirers when reasonably necessary for legitimate business operations.
  • Government authorities, law enforcement, or courts when disclosure is required by law or needed to protect rights and safety.

If Ember is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable law.

Your choices and controls

  • You may unsubscribe from marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in the message.
  • You may opt out of promotional texts using the instructions provided in the message.
  • You may manage cookies through your browser settings, though some site functions may be affected.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable law provides those rights.

If you submit a privacy request, we may need to verify your identity before completing it.

Security, retention, and children's privacy

Ember uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfillment, subscription administration, customer support, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and recordkeeping.

Ember's store is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Updates and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

For questions or privacy requests, contact us at support@shopemberco.com.
