Technique application — at-a-glance

H Habituation cadence

Three ingredient bullets get three distinct shapes (cause-effect, compound-consequence, maintenance). Pain-consequence beat breaks the source's three flat parallel sentences with a paired fragment + em-dash + one-word resolution + closing couplet. Proof stat triple deliberately stays parallel because it's percussive.

D Managed disfluency

Hook fragment cluster ("your drive. Your energy. Your edge."). Subject-drop on "Starts burning it instead." Em-dash setup → one-word punch ("They stall."). "Thinner — but foggier. Weaker." replaces the source's flowing comma list. Limited to roughly one move per beat — not every line.

R 6th-grade reading level

"Cortisol spike" → "stress hormones through the roof." "Rapid weight loss" → "losing weight that fast." "Tanks testosterone production at the source" / "lowers SHBG" / "blocks the enzyme" — all clinical mechanism language cut entirely (also a compliance call). Verbs from skill shortlist: supports, feeds, keeps.

V Brand voice / no-go

"Protect / fight back / destroy" → "built for this exact gap." "Win / not a win" → "trade you didn't agree to." "Stack" → "protocol." "Right now it's 50% off" → no countdown urgency. Direct T-production claims (cortisol→T, SHBG, aromatase) replaced with system-level support language (stress response, energy/endurance, circulation).

F Factual integrity

Mars Men's 8 ingredients → Tenor's 21. Mars Men's 91%/87% from 1,000-person survey → Tenor's 94%/87%/92% from 1,203-men survey. "50% off / money-back" → "52% off / $35/mo / 90-day empty-bottle guarantee." Doses added: Tongkat Ali 400mg, Maca 250mg, L-Arginine 250mg. Zinc/Vitamin D doses from source link description deleted (not Tenor's hero ingredients in this angle).

S Mobile staircase

Single ideas on single lines, blank lines as punctuation between sections. Pain-consequence beat opens up from one dense paragraph in the source to five line-broken moves. Stat triple stays tight (no blank lines between them — percussive). Negation couplet stays tight ("Not from a stimulant. Not from a needle.").
